**April 1–4, 2025 · London, England.**

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2025 brought adopters and technologists from
the open source and cloud native communities to London. CNCF-hosted co-located
events ran on April 1, followed by three days of keynotes, breakouts and the
Solutions Showcase on April 2–4.

- [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Europe 2025](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/archive/2025/kubecon-cloudnativecon-europe/)
- [Schedule](https://kccnceu2025.sched.com/)
- [Session recordings](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLj6h78yzYM2MP0QhYFK8HOb8UqgbIkLMc)

## Sessions from end user organizations

The event published no end user track, so the sessions below are those whose
speakers listed an organization that meets the
[CNCF End User definition](https://www.cncf.io/enduser/#end-user-definition)
as their company in the official schedule: an organization that uses cloud
native technologies internally rather than selling or supporting them. They
include the End User TAB town hall and the end user keynote panel alongside
talks from [CNCF End User members](/community/members) such as Adobe, Amadeus,
Apple, Bloomberg, Capital One, CERN, Decathlon, eBay, E.ON, G-Research,
Goldman Sachs, Intuit, JPMorgan Chase, LinkedIn, Mercedes-Benz, Michelin, Novo
Nordisk, PostFinance, Shopify, Spotify, Thales, The New York Times, Uber and
Volvo Cars, and from adopters across Europe and beyond: banks and insurers such
as Fidelity International, HDI Global, HSBC, Macquarie, Millennium bcp, Saxo
Bank and Swiss Re; public bodies and research organizations such as BWI,
CNAM, EUMETSAT, INFN, NAV, the Paul Scherrer Institute and SciLifeLab; media,
retail and travel companies such as Adevinta, DB Schenker, Elsevier, Etsy,
Forbes, Lidl, The LEGO Group and Wolters Kluwer; telecommunications operators
such as BT, Orange, Swisscom and Vodafone; technology, gaming and science
organizations such as ByteDance, CyberAgent, Gen, Meta, Miro, Netflix, NTT,
OPPO, Yelp and Zupee; and universities including Aalto, EPFL,
Giessen, Groningen, Masaryk, NYU, Oxford, Purdue, Trento, UIUC and Uppsala,
among others.
