**June 16–17, 2025 · Tokyo, Japan.**

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Japan 2025 brought adopters and technologists from
the open source and cloud native communities to Tokyo for two days of
keynotes, breakouts, lightning talks and the Solutions Showcase.

- [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon Japan 2025](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/archive/2025/kubecon-cloudnativecon-japan/)
- [Schedule](https://kccncjpn2025.sched.com/)
- [Session recordings](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLj6h78yzYM2PsNdcBGWR_mVN-SRLQl6KM)

## Sessions from end user organizations

The event published no end user track, so the sessions below are those whose
speakers listed an organization that meets the
[CNCF End User definition](https://www.cncf.io/enduser/#end-user-definition)
as their company in the official schedule: an organization that uses cloud
native technologies internally rather than selling or supporting them. They
include keynotes from Bloomberg, Canva, CyberAgent, Sony and Tokyo Gas
alongside talks from [CNCF End User members](/community/members) such as
Apple, Bloomberg, Cybozu, LY Corporation, Royal Bank of Canada and Toyota,
and from adopters such as Autify, Deutsche Bank, Mapbox, NTT, Preferred
Networks, Recruit, Sony Interactive Entertainment, Kyoto University, New York
University and the University of Tokyo.
