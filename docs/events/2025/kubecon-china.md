**June 10–11, 2025 · Hong Kong.**

KubeCon + CloudNativeCon China 2025 brought adopters and technologists from
the open source and cloud native communities to Hong Kong for two days of
keynotes, breakouts, lightning talks and the Solutions Showcase.

- [KubeCon + CloudNativeCon China 2025](https://events.linuxfoundation.org/archive/2025/kubecon-cloudnativecon-china/)
- [Schedule](https://kccncchn2025.sched.com/)
- [Session recordings](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLj6h78yzYM2P1xtALqTcCmRAa6142uERl)

## Sessions from end user organizations

The event published no end user track, so the sessions below are those whose
speakers listed an organization that meets the
[CNCF End User definition](https://www.cncf.io/enduser/#end-user-definition)
as their company in the official schedule: an organization that uses cloud
native technologies internally rather than selling or supporting them. They
include keynotes from 5miles, Bilibili, eBay, HKGAI and iFlytek alongside
talks from [CNCF End User members](/community/members) such as Ant Group,
Bloomberg and eBay, and from adopters such as ByteDance, China Mobile,
Horizon Robotics, KPMG Australia, Kuaishou, PayPal, vivo and Xiaohongshu, plus
educators from the Hong Kong Institute of Information Technology.
